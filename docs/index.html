Source: Salford Parks Report Books (1874–1967), University of Salford Archives & Special Collections. Reproduced with permission from Salford Community Leisure.

Transcriptions on this site are first-pass, AI-generated drafts produced from the scanned page images, shown to demonstrate what machine transcription and analysis can do with the collection. Uncertain readings are marked [?]; they have not yet been checked against the originals and should not be cited as definitive.
